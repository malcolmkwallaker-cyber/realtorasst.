# Social Media Content Generator

Build a month of social content the business can actually post.

**Input:** website URL plus optional platform focus (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok/Reels, X). If not specified, recommend the 1–2 platforms that fit the business type (e.g. Instagram + Facebook for local/med-spa/real estate; LinkedIn for B2B).

## Steps

1. **Research:** fetch the site; extract offer, audience, location (if local), proof points, and visual assets implied (before/afters, listings, team). Search for what similar accounts post successfully.
2. **Content pillars:** define 4–5 pillars with a target mix, e.g. education 40%, proof/results 25%, behind-the-scenes 15%, offer 10%, engagement 10%.
3. **30-day calendar:** a table — Day | Pillar | Format (reel, carousel, story, post) | Hook | CTA.
4. **Write the first 10 posts in full:**
   - **Hook** (first line that stops the scroll)
   - **Caption** (skimmable, line breaks, one CTA)
   - **Visual direction** (what to film/photograph — one line)
   - **Hashtags** (8–15, mix of local/niche/broad where relevant)
5. Use `[PLACEHOLDER: …]` for client-specific facts (real results, names, prices). Never invent testimonials or statistics.

## Output

Save to `marketing-reports/<domain>/social-calendar-<date>.md` and show in chat: the pillars, the calendar table, and the 10 complete posts.
